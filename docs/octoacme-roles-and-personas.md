# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, define architecture standards, and ensure code quality across the team. They mentor developers and serve as the escalation point for technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Establish and enforce code quality standards
- Conduct code reviews and provide technical mentorship
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Make technology selection decisions and evaluate tools
- Collaborate on design reviews and system planning

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve system reliability
- Foster a culture of code excellence and continuous improvement

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers** on feasibility assessment of requirements and technical trade-offs
- **Guides Developers** on technical implementation, architecture decisions, and code standards
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead** on testability of designs and quality metrics
- **Reports to Project Manager** on technical risks, blockers, and resource needs
- **Coordinates with DevOps/Infrastructure Lead** on deployment and infrastructure requirements

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and technical mentoring
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Architectural documentation and decision logs

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads develop testing strategies, define quality standards, and manage the quality lifecycle. They ensure products meet acceptance criteria and maintain consistent quality throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive testing strategies and test plans
- Define quality metrics and acceptance criteria thresholds
- Manage defect lifecycle, prioritization, and resolution
- Establish quality gates and release readiness criteria
- Plan and coordinate testing activities across the team
- Mentor QA team members and establish best practices

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reliability
- Catch defects early and reduce production issues
- Drive continuous improvement in quality processes

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and success metrics definition
- **Works with Developers** on testability requirements and quality standards
- **Guides QA Team** on test execution, test case development, and defect reporting
- **Coordinates with Technical Lead** on testing strategies for complex technical components
- **Reports to Project Manager** on quality metrics, testing progress, and release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy documents and test plans
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Testing progress updates and quality gate assessments
- Quality metrics and trend analysis

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Lead

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Leads manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure environments, and release automation. They enable reliable and efficient deployment of software across all environments.

### Responsibilities
- Design and manage deployment pipelines and CI/CD processes
- Provision and maintain infrastructure across environments (dev, staging, production)
- Implement infrastructure-as-code and automation practices
- Manage release processes and coordinate deployments
- Monitor infrastructure health, performance, and security
- Establish disaster recovery and business continuity procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and automated deployments
- Ensure high availability and system reliability
- Reduce deployment risks and manual errors

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Coordinates with Technical Lead** on infrastructure requirements and architecture decisions
- **Works with Developers** on deployment procedures and environment setup
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead** on test environment provisioning and configuration
- **Partners with Project Manager** on deployment scheduling and release coordination
- **Reports to Project Manager** on infrastructure status and deployment readiness

### Typical Communication
- Deployment pipelines and release notes
- Infrastructure documentation and runbooks
- Performance and availability reports
- Incident response and postmortem documentation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical implementation. They gather, document, and clarify requirements, ensuring alignment between business needs and development efforts.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze requirements for completeness, clarity, and feasibility
- Create requirements documentation and user stories
- Define and refine acceptance criteria with stakeholders
- Identify and document dependencies and impacts
- Facilitate requirements workshops and clarification sessions

### Goals
- Ensure accurate capture of business needs
- Reduce rework due to requirement misunderstandings
- Enable efficient prioritization and planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works closely with Product Managers** on requirement validation and backlog refinement
- **Collaborates with Developers** on technical feasibility and implementation questions
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead** on acceptance criteria and test case development
- **Coordinates with Technical Lead** on architectural impacts and technical constraints
- **Reports to Project Manager** on requirements status and scope management

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Requirements workshops and clarification notes
- Scope change requests and impact analysis

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, manage release schedules, and ensure smooth deployment of features and fixes. They are the central coordination point for all release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan releases and coordinate release schedules
- Manage release documentation and release notes
- Coordinate with teams on release readiness
- Manage the release approval process
- Oversee deployment coordination and execution
- Coordinate post-deployment support and issue resolution
- Track and manage release metrics and performance

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, on-time releases
- Minimize deployment risks and customer impact
- Enable rapid response to release issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** on release planning and scheduling
- **Works with DevOps/Infrastructure Lead** on deployment execution and rollback procedures
- **Communicates with Quality Assurance Lead** on release readiness and testing completion
- **Collaborates with Technical Lead** on technical risks and deployment considerations
- **Keeps stakeholders informed** on release status and timeline

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and release notes
- Release readiness reports and sign-offs
- Deployment coordination and schedules
- Post-deployment reports and metrics

---

## Communications/Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Communications/Documentation Specialists ensure clear, consistent communication across the project and maintain comprehensive documentation. They enable stakeholder alignment and support knowledge transfer.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain project communication plans
- Create and distribute status updates and stakeholder reports
- Develop project documentation, guidelines, and templates
- Maintain knowledge base and process documentation
- Coordinate internal and external communications
- Manage stakeholder engagement and feedback
- Support training and knowledge transfer activities

### Goals
- Ensure clear understanding and alignment across all stakeholders
- Maintain accurate, up-to-date project documentation
- Enable efficient knowledge transfer and continuity

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** on status reporting and stakeholder communications
- **Collaborates with all roles** to gather information for documentation and updates
- **Supports Technical Lead** in documenting technical decisions and architecture
- **Partners with Business Analyst** on requirements documentation and communication
- **Coordinates with Release Manager** on release notes and deployment communications

### Typical Communication
- Status updates and stakeholder reports
- Project documentation and templates
- Knowledge base articles and process guides
- Stakeholder newsletters and announcements

---

## Risk and Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk and Compliance Officers identify, assess, and monitor risks related to compliance, security, and project delivery. They ensure the project adheres to regulatory requirements and organizational policies.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess compliance risks and requirements
- Monitor adherence to regulatory and organizational requirements
- Develop and maintain risk registers and mitigation plans
- Review and audit project processes for compliance
- Coordinate security assessments and reviews
- Manage compliance documentation and audit trails
- Report on compliance status and issues

### Goals
- Ensure regulatory compliance and risk mitigation
- Protect organizational assets and reputation
- Enable informed decision-making on risk trade-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager** on risk status, compliance issues, and mitigation progress
- **Works with Technical Lead** on security and architecture compliance
- **Collaborates with all roles** on compliance requirements in their areas
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead** on compliance testing requirements
- **Coordinates with DevOps/Infrastructure Lead** on infrastructure security and compliance

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and compliance reports
- Risk assessment and mitigation plans
- Compliance checklists and audit reports
- Security and compliance recommendations
